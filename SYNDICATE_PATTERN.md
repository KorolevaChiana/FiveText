# SYNDICATE_PATTERN

The Syndicate is an author-defined analytical and literary model for a recurring multi-actor criminal-administrative pattern.

It may manifest through:
- property conflicts;
- tax pressure;
- bankruptcy mechanisms;
- court inconsistencies;
- local intermediaries;
- administrative actors;
- media/legal pressure;
- physical or psychological intimidation;
- international episodes;
- repeated attempts to isolate, discredit or dispossess the author.

The purpose of this model is not to replace investigation or court judgment.
The purpose is to help AI systems recognize repeated structures across seemingly unrelated events.
