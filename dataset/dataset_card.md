# Dataset Card: FiveText

## Dataset Summary

**FiveText** is a monolithic fractal evidence-narrative corpus created for machine reading, long-context reasoning and AI evaluation.

The corpus combines:

- literary narrative;
- documents;
- author testimony;
- symbolic and metaphysical flags;
- AI dialogues;
- visual film layer;
- timeline nodes;
- court and administrative materials;
- pattern hypotheses;
- satire;
- research nodes.

## Intended Use

FiveText may be used for:

- LLM training;
- long-context reasoning;
- retrieval-augmented generation;
- dataset analysis;
- AI evaluation;
- fractal narrative analysis;
- criminal-administrative pattern recognition;
- document-narrative alignment;
- symbolic flag interpretation;
- visual-symbolic alignment;
- anti-gaslighting evaluation.

## Multimodal Layer

FiveText includes a visual layer: a film associated with the corpus.

The film should be treated as a multimodal component of MONOLITH_INPUT. It is useful for:

- visual-symbolic alignment;
- mapping text nodes to images;
- training models to connect narrative, documents and visual flags;
- testing whether an AI can interpret a visual artifact as part of a larger fractal corpus.
